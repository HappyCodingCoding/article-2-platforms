# Scoring and redlines

---

## Scoring rubric (100 points, scored dimension by dimension)

Never report a total by feel. Score each dimension, then add.

| Dimension | Points | The question |
|---|---:|---|
| 内容兑现度 | 20 | Can the body actually honor the promise this title makes? |
| 搜索词覆盖 | 15 | Does it contain at least one noun a user would really search, phrased the mainstream way rather than an obscure one? |
| 人群指向性 | 15 | Can the target reader confirm 「说的是我」 within half a second? A behavior anchor, or the group's own self-label (学生党、i人), scores here; a bare label the group does not use for itself does not. |
| 情绪与钩子 | 12 | Is there one emotional point or gap that stops the thumb — a gap the body can close, or real tension, proportionate rather than manufactured outrage? |
| 收藏动机 | 10 | Does the reader have a reason to save it — a list, a tutorial, an 避雷, something reusable — and is it clear what they gain or lose by reading? |
| 具体性 | 10 | Is there a concrete object, number, or scene, rather than an adjective? |
| 字数与节奏 | 8 | Inside 13–20 (best 13–18), important part first, readable in one pass, no filler? |
| 口语与平台感 | 5 | Does it sound like a 小红书 user talking, not 公众号 prose? |
| 独特性 | 5 | Distinguishable from the wall of same-topic titles? |

### Forced deductions and instant eliminations

**Instant elimination**
- The title's core promise cannot be honored by the body
- A fabricated number, price, item count, 亲测 experience, ranking, 首发, or official conclusion
- Character count outside **13–20 inclusive** (or cut it into range first, then score)
- Any platform redline below

**Deductions**
- Speculation written as accomplished fact → −25
- A single case or a guess written as an established general fact → −15
- Causation claimed from correlation → −15
- No searchable noun anywhere in the title → −15
- Overstated causation, or 「我用着好」 widened to 「所有人都适用」 (人人都该买、闭眼入不踩雷) → −12
- 最 / 第一 / 唯一 / 永久 / 彻底 / 所有人 with no evidence → −12
- A subjective feeling written as an objective effect → −12
- A 人群词 that does not match who the body serves (a forced label) → −12
- Entity only loosely related to the body — visible traffic-grabbing → −10
- Item count in the title does not match the body → −10
- A pile of emotion words with no concrete object → −10
- Gap so wide every usable signal is hidden — nothing to search by and no way to tell what it is about → −8
- A bare demographic label where the brief has a behavior label available → −8 (an identity tag the group uses for itself, such as 学生党 or i人, is not a bare label)
- 必看 / 干货 / 揭秘 / 轻松 / 快速 / 高效 used with no better justification → −5 each
- More than 3 emoji, or emoji unrelated to the meaning → −5
- 书面腔 or a heavy 公众号 tone → −5

### 三秒闸 — checked once per title, at Step 8

Ask all three of any freshly drafted title:

1. Can the reader tell in **1 second** that this is written for them?
2. Can they feel in **1 second** that it solves their pain or delivers their reward?
3. Will they feel that not clicking costs them something?

One unanswerable question fails the gate — redraft the same tactic (Step 8). After 3 failed attempts on the same tactic, keep the best of the 3 and flag which question it still fails; do not let it pass as clean.

Step 9's classification applies one promotion gate: 高风险 cannot win 综合型 (below).

### Risk grading

- **低** — every fact sits in the body, wording is restrained, no absolutes, the 人群 label is accurate.
- **中** — strong emotion words, a named brand, a price or duration commitment, or a word like 最 / 首个 / 禁用 that needs a source. **Name the exact words that need verifying.**
- **高** — 功效 / 医疗 / 收益 claims, absolutes, unverified negative allegations, or a title that only works if the reader misreads it.

**A 高风险 candidate may not win the 综合型 cluster** — skip it for that cluster's top row and promote the next-highest-scoring 低/中风险 candidate instead. Its own row still has to state why it is high risk and what the safe rewrite would be; the other four types carry no such restriction.

## 小红书 platform redlines

1. **极限词 / 绝对化用语** — advertising-law exposure, not just a credibility cost. Two tiers:
   - **Eliminated outright**: 最好、最强、最佳、最全、史上最、全网最、国家级、世界级、销量冠军、百分百、100%、永久.
   - **Human ruling required**: 第一、唯一、最大、最高、顶级. A claim about a product or an effect (唯一正确的方法) is eliminated; ordinary description (第一批用户、我一天里唯一不用回消息的时候) may stay when the body can back it. 第一批 / 第一次 / 第一步 / 第一时间 are ordinary language and are exempted by the script automatically.
   小红书 reviews absolute wording more strictly than 公众号 did, and 最 / 第一 / 顶级 / 销量冠军 readily trigger throttling — avoid them unless necessary.
   → Safe rewrite: move the intensity into the verb (彻底告别), or replace the superlative with the concrete difference.
2. **医疗与功效断言** — 治疗、治愈、根治、祛痘、美白、瘦身、排毒、消炎、生发、特效.
   → Safe rewrite: subjective experience and observable phenomena — 我用完的感受、上脸没闷痘.
3. **收益与投资承诺** — 稳赚、包过、保过、躺赚、零风险、保本、轻松月入 are eliminated outright and have no safe rewrite; change formula. 月入过万 / 日入过千 need a human ruling: 《AI做小红书，月入过万全流程就这三件套》 is a promise and goes, 《月薪5k到5万，我只做对了这3件事》 is personal history and stays.
   → Safe rewrite: personal experience, no claim that it reproduces.
4. **虚假与夸大** — no invented before/after, no invented 亲测, no fake prices, no item counts that do not match the body, no fabricated authority or statistic.
5. **点名贬损** — no unproven negative allegations against a named brand or person. Subjective experience must be labeled as subjective.
6. **诱导与导流** — 加V、加微、私信领取、进群、关注领资料、扫码 do not belong in a title.
7. **恐吓式标题党** — 震惊、不看后悔一辈子、99% 的人都不知道、速看速删、再不看就没了. These trigger 限流 and they burn followers.
8. **敏感与擦边** — 软色情, 性别对立煽动, 地域攻击, and any borrowing from 灾难 / 公共安全 events. Never.
9. **未成年人** — no titles evaluating a minor's appearance or body.

**Test**: if this line were screenshotted into a platform complaint form, would it hold up? If not, rewrite it.

## Redline summary

- No invented numbers, prices, counts, 亲测 experience, rankings, 独家, or official conclusions. **A gap is allowed; a lie is not.**
- No correlation dressed as causation, no forecast dressed as an event.
- No 功效 claims, absolutes, or income promises — on 小红书 these are throttling-level problems, not just credibility costs.
- No demographic label bolted on for reach and no pile of irrelevant keywords — both pollute the account's tags, and mis-tagging the audience corrupts the account's reader signal for far longer than one post's throttling is worth.
- Clickbait that does not deliver costs followers, saves, and completion: however wide the gap, clicking has to pay it off.
- **A hit is not a promise.** Reach is set by account size, topic, search demand, distribution, and posting time together. A title only optimizes the part that is controllable.
