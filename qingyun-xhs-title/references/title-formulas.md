# Formula layer — 26 formulas

The psychological trigger decides which nerve to touch. The formula decides the sentence shape 小红书 readers are used to being asked in.

Every formula below is laid out the same way: **公式 → core logic → slot breakdown → 范例 (verified in budget) → its AI 废稿 → the de-AI tactic specific to this formula → redline.**

The 废稿 line is not decoration. Each one is a real, plausible title that would be produced for the same subject by defaulting to 泛人群 + 通用话术 + 空悬念. Read it before writing the 爆款 version — the failure mode is far easier to recognize than the success mode is to invent.

**废稿 examples are diagnostic material, and they stay here.** They exist so you can catch the pattern in your own drafts and drop those drafts before they reach the candidate list. Never write one into the output, and never present a recommendation next to a deliberately weak twin — the user asked for titles worth publishing, not a demonstration.

Every 范例 is a real title seen in 小红书 search results, carries its character count, and has been run through `scripts/script.py`. Counts are for the **published form**, with no decorative spaces around numbers or Latin words — those spaces cost budget like any other character. A formula with no 范例 has none because no real title that fit it was found; it is not a sign the formula fails on 小红书.

---

## Formula parts and the 13–20 character budget

Each formula below states its own **公式** — a short pattern of two or three parts — and elaborates it into a **Slots** breakdown: what goes in each part, and the test for whether it's earning its place. There is no single part count shared by every formula. 痛点直击型 has three parts; 热点绑定型 has two, divided by a colon rather than a separate third part. The part count is always whatever that formula's own 公式 states — never a generic shape imposed from outside it.

Whatever the count, together the filled parts earn the 13–20 character budget. A three-part formula earns the full range; a two-part formula honestly earns less unless one part is doing unusually concrete work. Never pad a thin part with adjectives to reach 13, and never cut a whole part to fit under 20 — trim a modifier instead.

**Density calibration**: a concrete entity does more work than a number. Anchor an entity first; a number earns its place only when the gap between two of them is the hook. Stacking four brand names in one line is a failure mode, not density.

## Real titles vs the 13–20 budget

Real 小红书 titles seen in search results run from a handful of characters to the 20-character cap, and the ones over the cap show up cut off. This skill mandates 13–20, so a popular title is often **outside the budget while still being the correct model for its formula** — the formula is what to copy, the length is not.

Both directions have a standard repair.

**Under 13 — the title is missing a slot, not missing words.** Never pad with adjectives; that is exactly the AI tell. 补槽后 below is an illustration built for this page, not a real title.

| 真实标题 | 字数 | 补槽后（示意） | 字数 | 补的是 |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| 油皮粉底液壁垒 | 7 | 油皮粉底液壁垒：这3款上脸不卡粉 | 16 | 结果预期（补上凭什么成壁垒） |
| 高中生慎买！ | 6 | 高中生慎买这5样文具，越用越后悔 | 16 | 具体对象 + 后果 |
| 宿舍坏物榜 | 5 | 宿舍坏物榜｜这5样买完最后悔 | 14 | 数字 + 后悔的理由 |

**Over 20 — cut modifiers, never cut a slot.** The platform truncates past 20, so the tail is simply lost. Two real titles as displayed, both cut mid-sentence: 《停了孩子3个兴趣班后，我悟了：中产家庭最》 and 《大班退掉了所有兴趣班，空出来的时间只做一》 — the payoff sat in the words that never showed. The fix is to shorten the front (后，我悟了、所有) so the slot that carries the payoff fits inside 20; never to drop that slot.

## 1. 痛点直击型 — highest hit rate, works in every category

**公式**：精准人群锚定 + 反常识痛点 + 强结果预期 / 灵魂反问

**标签前置变体**：`[人群词]｜[他们的具体处境] + [结果]` — put the group's own self-label first so the right reader claims it in half a second and the platform files the note in the right tag pool.

**Core logic**: lock onto a specific reader, name the anxiety they live with in one line, and produce 「这说的不就是我吗」. They click to find the answer.

**Slots**
- 精准人群锚定 = a group defined by **shared behavior**, never by demographic. This is the whole formula.
- 反常识痛点 = the thing they do every day, correctly as they believe, and still get no result from — the 「我明明这么做了，为什么还是不行」 anxiety.
- 强结果预期 / 灵魂反问 = exactly what clicking gets them, so 「看完也没用」 never crosses their mind.

**范例**
- 《写给焦虑的新手妈妈，熬过三个月真的会变好》（20 字，人群锚定 + 痛点 + 结果预期三槽全满，是这个预算的上限样本）
- 《24毕业gap两年找不到工作每天都想哭》（19 字，人群锚定与痛点合并成一句现状）
- 《当发现自己升不上去又涨不了薪时》（15 字，结果预期缺位，靠句尾悬而未决留住读者）
- 《油痘肌避雷｜植村秀小方瓶真实上脸状态》（18 字，标签前置变体：人群词｜处境）
- 《大一开学只花300元就搞定的宿舍好物》（18 字，标签前置变体：人群词 + 预算 + 结果）
- 《准大一新生必看！宿舍上床下桌改造全过程～》（20 字，标签前置变体：人群词打头，再给处境）

**AI 废稿**：《新手妈妈必看的带娃情绪管理技巧》（15 字）— 泛人群、无痛点、无预期，三个槽位全空，必看 和 技巧 只是把字数撑到区间里。**字数进了区间从来不说明槽位满了。**

**De-AI tactic**: never 必看 / 干货 / 揭秘. Lock the person with a concrete behavior, hit them with something they are living through right now. The first reaction must be 「这说的不就是我吗？」 — not 「这好像跟我有点关系」.

**Redline**: the behavior label must describe the audience the content genuinely serves. Bolting on a high-traffic group the article does not serve poisons the account's reader signal for far longer than one post's reach is worth — mis-tagging the audience lowers the account's tag precision and is worse than having no tag at all.

## 2. 反常识颠覆型 — first choice for 观点文 and 认知文, breaks out easily

**公式**：反常识结论 + 反差冲突 + 为什么 / 凭什么

**纠错变体**：`原来 / 其实 + [常见做法] + 是错的` 或 `别再 [常见动作] 了` — overturn a habit the reader assumes is right, so the unease is 「我是不是一直做错了」.

**Core logic**: break a settled belief, create 「意料之外」, and make them click to see 「凭什么这么说」.

**Slots**
- 反常识结论 = the belief itself — **the more routinely people recite it as correct, the more it is worth reversing.**
- 反差冲突 = a concrete, checkable outcome holding the reversal up (停了兴趣班 → 成绩年级前三), so it cannot be waved away as 胡说.
- 为什么 / 凭什么 = stop right after the conclusion. Leave the argument in the body and make them click to argue with you.

**范例**
- 《别再感动自己了！你假装努力终究瞒不过结果》（20 字，直接冲着读者昨天的做法去）
- 《最危险的努力：每天都在学习，却几年没变》（19 字，几年没变 是撑住反转的可查结果）
- 《一年兴趣班砍掉一半，我反而轻松了》（16 字，动作 + 反差结果，为什么 / 凭什么留在正文）
- 《别再抬头了😭头前伸这招亲测管用》（15 字，纠错变体：别再 + 常见动作）

**AI 废稿**：《理财的重要性：如何提升你的收入》（15 字）— runs with the consensus, no conflict, no reason to click. 的重要性 and 如何 are the two most reliable markers of a title with no position in it.

**De-AI tactic**: AI writes fake reversals — 「你以为 A，其实是 B」 where B is another piece of correct-sounding filler. A real reversal has to offend the reader's own habits: what they did yesterday, what they just paid for, what they just posted. Tell them that was wrong and they will click to fight you.

**Redline**: the reversal must come from fact, not from a wish to be contrarian. The body has to supply the mechanism, not restate the phenomenon.

## 3. 利益前置型 — mandatory for 干货文, 副业文, 教程文

**公式**：低成本投入 + 高回报结果 + 可复制 / 零门槛承诺

**时间承诺变体**：`[时长] + [投入] + [结果]` — a concrete time scale makes the result feel reachable (`每天10分钟，一个月的变化`). The duration and the result must come from your own experience, and never promise that anyone else will reproduce them.

**Core logic**: state the payoff up front. Minimum cost against maximum return lowers the decision threshold to 「花 5 分钟看完就赚到」.

**Slots**
- 低成本投入 = the cost, written as a number they can pay right now — 10 分钟、3 个方法、不用辞职. Never 只要坚持, which has no scale.
- 高回报结果 = a return they can convert immediately into money, time, or identity.
- 可复制 / 零门槛承诺 = 零基础也能 / 直接套用, which pre-empts 「我大概不行」.

**范例**
- 《每天10分钟，四个动作无痛直腿养成计划》（19 字，成本写成读者现在就付得起的数字）
- 《网购换这些关键词，直接省一半！》（15 字，成本是换关键词这一个动作，回报是省一半）
- 《不用去瑜伽馆，6个动作在家悄悄变美🌈》（18 字，先拿掉一个门槛，再报出动作数）

**AI 废稿**：《掌握这些方法，轻松学好英语不用愁》（16 字）— vague cost, vague return, no promise. 这些 is a cost slot with nothing in it.

**De-AI tactic**: 轻松 / 快速 / 高效 are AI's default filler and readers are immune to them. **Replace adjectives with numbers** — 轻松学英语 is noise, 每天 10 分钟 is a commitment. The more concrete the gap between cost and return, the stronger the title.

**Redline**: a real title in this shape is 《AI做小红书，月入过万全流程就这三件套》 — and 月入过万 is a **收益承诺 that this skill's compliance gate stops**. The formula is right, that payoff is not. Write the return as your own experience with no claim it reproduces (我靠它多挣出一份房租), or pick another formula. 包过 / 稳赚 / 零风险 are never available.

## 4. 数字聚焦型 — works everywhere, lifts CTR

**公式**：数字 + 核心主题 + 价值 / 悬念

**收藏指令变体**：`[数字] + [品类 / 方法] + [建议收藏 / 码住 / 直接抄]` — a countable amount plus an explicit save word activates the save, the strongest positive signal here (see 清单 in `references/psych-triggers.md`).

**Core logic**: numbers carry certainty, impact, and structure. They tell the reader the size of what they are buying before they spend attention on it.

**Slots**
- 数字 = the number itself. **Prefer small and odd (2, 3, 5)** — a large number reads as padding, a round number reads as guesswork.
- 核心主题 = the number must hang on something concrete (4 个风口、2 件事), never on an abstraction.
- 价值 / 悬念 = one more cut after the number — either value (错过再等 5 年) or suspense (我只做对了 2 件事).

**范例**
- 《月薪5k到5万，我只做对了这3件事》（17 字，数字聚焦 + 悬念钩子 stacked）
- 《想赚300万，你每天重复6小时就行》（17 字，300 万对 6 小时就是落差本身）
- 《宿舍8大智商税‼️买完后悔四年》（14 字，数字挂在具体主题上，后果是第二刀）
- 《控糖要做的➡️8个抗阻运动✨建议收藏》（17 字，收藏指令变体）

**AI 废稿**：《提升自我的10个实用方法，建议收藏》（17 字）— the number hangs on an empty concept, no value, no suspense, and 10 个 is the exact size AI defaults to.

**De-AI tactic**: AI reaches for 10 个技巧 and 15 个方法; the bigger the number, the more it reads as filler. What actually pulls is the **gap between two numbers** — 《月薪5k到5万，我只做对了这3件事》: the big number is the temptation, the small one is the feasibility, and the distance between them is the reason to click.

**Redline**: the count must match the body exactly. 3 个方法 over an article with four is a broken promise the reader notices immediately; 7 个 in the title over 4 in the body, next to a save cue, is fishing for saves and earns bad comments. 月薪5k到5万 survives only as personal history, never as a promise.

## 5. 情绪共鸣型 — the code for 情感文, 中年感悟, 群体话题

**公式**：群体标签 + 情绪戳点 + 共情金句

**Core logic**: lock a group, say the thing they have been holding in, and turn recognition into a forward.

**Slots**
- 群体标签 = the label they use for **themselves** (80 后、成年人、全职妈妈) — an identity that already existed before this article, not a term the piece is coining right now — so they claim it on sight. **This is the one formula where a bare group label is correct, but only when the label predates the article**; a term the piece just invented, however catchy, has not been claimed by anyone yet and still needs the behavior anchor every other formula requires. The de-AI gate will still flag a legitimate bare label, and this is the exemption to cite.
- 情绪戳点 = an emotion they carry but have never said out loud — 不敢病、不敢停、崩溃时的静音.
- 共情金句 = write that emotion as a line worth screenshotting, so the reaction is 「你替我说了」 rather than 「我看完了」.

**范例**
- 《成年人的世界：深夜崩溃，天亮自愈》（16 字）
- 《当妈后，我发现“累”真的很难被看见😔》（18 字）
- 《当妈哪有不疯的？又哪有不会变好的？》（17 字）

**AI 废稿**：《新手妈妈的情绪压力与调适方法》（14 字）— vague label, abstract emotion, no line anyone would screenshot. 压力 and 调适 are emotions and fixes **named** rather than felt.

**De-AI tactic**: AI **names** emotions — 焦虑、压力、疲惫 — but people **feel** them through scenes and actions. Not 新手妈妈压力大 but 当妈哪有不疯的？又哪有不会变好的？. Not 成年人压力大 but 深夜崩溃，天亮自愈. Swap nouns for verbs and the emotion lands.

**Redline**: resonance, never manufactured group anxiety, humiliation, or stigma. Do not set one group against another for the click.

## 6. 悬念钩子型 — first choice for 故事文, 人物文, 揭秘文

**公式**：强情绪极端场景 / 反常细节 + 灵魂拷问 + 留白悬念

**悬念缺口变体**：`[人群] + [强评价] + 不点明对象` — say only that something is very good, or that something happened, and withhold what it is (`怎么会有这么香的上铺宿舍～`, `打工人必备的这个App，我藏不住了`).

**Core logic**: say half. Put out something abnormal, conflicted, and consequential, withhold the answer, and the information gap does the rest.

**Slots**
- 强情绪极端场景 / 反常细节 = the most conflicted, most charged outcome in the story, moved to the front.
- 灵魂拷问 = the question they turn over privately, which carries them from 「主角的事」 to 「我的事」.
- 留白悬念 = the action without the result. Hook, no answer.

**范例**
- 《当着办公室所有人的面骂了业务面试官》（17 字）
- 《好消息：被阿里捞了。坏消息：》（14 字，把结果整句留白）
- 《怎么会有这么香的上铺宿舍～》（13 字，悬念缺口变体：评价在前，但保留了搜索词 上铺宿舍）
- 《山姆夯品！这么哇塞怎么没人说啊！！》（17 字，悬念缺口变体：强评价在前，对象只点到 山姆夯品）
- 《没人和你说的体制内新手任务plus版》（18 字，悬念缺口变体：藏起内容，保留 体制内新手 这个搜索词）

**Contrast worth studying**: 《被面试官骂过之后，我才明白职场真相》（17 字，an illustration written for this page, not a real title）is the same subject as the first line above and comfortably in budget — but 真相 hides something generic, while 当着办公室所有人的面骂了业务面试官 is an abnormal action whose missing result is exactly what the reader now wants. **Same formula, same length range, completely different hook strength.** Length was never the variable.

**AI 废稿**：《面试失败3次后，我才明白这个道理》（16 字）— in budget and still dead: no emotion, no conflict, and 这个道理 withholds nothing anybody wanted. **This is the clearest proof that hitting 13–20 is a gate, not an achievement.**

**De-AI tactic**: refuse flat narration and never 我有一个朋友. Use a specific, charged scene and a specific action so the reader feels the state of mind rather than watching gossip go past. **The gap has to be over something they want** — hiding a generic 道理 is not suspense.

**Redline**: an over-wide gap that hides every usable signal reads as clickbait and converts worse than a half-open one. The body must answer the exact question the title raised, in the opening. On 小红书 use pure suspense with care: hiding every keyword also gives up the search traffic, so a suspense title must keep at least one searchable term.

## 7. 热点绑定型 — fastest route to reach, borrowed

**公式**：热点事件 / 名人 IP + 核心观点 / 价值关联

**节点变体**：`[节点词] + [人群 / 预算] + [具体产出]` — hang the note on a time node everyone is searching right now (`国庆去哪儿人少，看这份大数据吧！`, `国庆别再人挤人了！江浙沪这几个小城真香！`). Pair it with 社会证明型 (#19) when 「别人已经开始了」 adds pressure to act.

**Core logic**: borrow attention that already exists, then bind it to your own vertical so the traffic is not spent on a subject you never cover.

**Slots** — this formula has **two** parts, not three, divided by a hinge rather than a separate middle piece. On 小红书 the hinge is usually 爆火背后 or 爆火给…的启示 rather than a colon.
- 热点事件 / 名人 IP = the specific name or event being searched right now. The more specific, the better the targeting.
- 核心观点 / 价值关联 = everything after the hinge — why this event has anything to do with the reader personally, rather than a spectacle they watch and leave. The hinge itself is the pivot: 热点 is the doorway, 观点 is what gets remembered.

**范例**
- 《《牛来》爆火给普通人的3条启示》（15 字）
- 《《牛来》爆火背后，当下大众真实的社会心态》（20 字）
- 《国庆别再人挤人了！江浙沪这几个小城真香！》（20 字，节点变体：节点词 + 别再 + 具体产出）
- 《国庆去哪儿人少，看这份大数据吧！》（16 字，节点变体：节点词 + 问题 + 依据）

**AI 废稿**：《最近的热点事件，我们能学到什么》（15 字）— the 热点 is never named, so nobody searches it and nobody has a stance to react to. 我们能学到什么 is an empty 核心观点 pretending to be a hook.

**De-AI tactic**: AI retells the event and produces a position-free news summary. **热点 takes the first half, 观点 takes the second, and the hinge is the border.** The first half borrows traffic; the second half is what you get remembered for. The 热点 expires, the 观点 does not — which is why the second half is worth more of the 20 characters than the first.

**Redline**: 灾难、公共安全、社会悲剧 are never borrowed from. The connection to your content must be real, not a name dropped for reach. A node word alone (`国庆3天`) is too short for the 13 floor, so the 节点变体 always needs one more filled slot (人群, 预算, or 具体产出).

## 8. 权威背书型 — for 干货文, 科普文, 专业文; buys trust fast

**公式**：权威身份 / 数据结论 + 核心主题 + 价值预期

**前提**：正文里要有真实可查的权威来源或数据 — 没有就换别的公式，不能靠编一个机构或统计数字来凑。

**Core logic**: borrow a source the reader already trusts and the credibility question is settled before they start reading.

**Slots**
- 权威身份 / 数据结论 = a source they already believe — 哈佛、纳瓦尔、大数据.
- 核心主题 = immediately narrow to one specific problem; never stop at 值得每个人学习.
- 价值预期 = what they will be able to do afterwards — 彻底告别拖延症、越早养成越好.

**范例**
- 《纳瓦尔：停止学AI提示词！培养这2种能力》（20 字，署名的权威 + 收窄的主题 + 具体的价值）
- 《哈佛：发疯式努力真的可以改命》（14 字，权威只占两个字，剩下的字数全给主题和价值）

**AI 废稿**：《专家教你如何做好时间管理，效率翻倍》（17 字）— unnamed authority, unbounded topic, no payoff.

**De-AI tactic**: authority needs a name and a source. 专家表示 and 研究显示 are AI's verbal tics and readers scroll straight past them; 哈佛 and 纳瓦尔 are actual backing.

**Redline**: **the authority and the data have to exist.** Inventing an institution or a statistic is not a credibility cost, it is a platform violation. If the source cannot be verified, use a different formula.

## 9. 内幕揭秘型 — for 新闻、产品更新, exposes a quiet move

**公式**：知名主体 + 偷偷 / 暗中做了什么 + 让人不安的目的

**Core logic**: expose something a powerful, recognized party did quietly, with a motive that unsettles the reader — the reveal itself, plus why it should worry them, is the hook.

**Slots**
- 知名主体 = a real, verifiable entity (公司、平台、机构) — never 有人 or 某平台. The vaguer the actor, the weaker the reveal.
- 偷偷 / 暗中做了什么 = a concrete, checkable action taken quietly — not a vague accusation.
- 让人不安的目的 = the motive or consequence that makes the reveal matter to the reader personally, stated but not over-explained.

**范例**
- None yet: no real 小红书 title collected both names the entity and points at a checkable quiet action, and the near misses were rumor rather than reporting.

**AI 废稿**：《揭秘某平台背后不为人知的秘密内幕》（16 字）— 主体是"某平台"、动作是"不为人知的秘密内幕"，两个槽位都空，揭秘 和 内幕 只是把字数垫进区间。

**De-AI tactic**: never let 揭秘 / 内幕 alone carry the title. Name the real entity and the real action; the discomfort must come from a checkable specific, not from the word 揭秘 doing the work.

**Redline**: the hidden action and its motive must be independently verifiable — reported, documented, or backed by the body's own evidence. An accusation the body cannot back up is a 点名贬损 violation, not a stylistic risk.

---

## 10. 冲突对立型 — for 争议、政策、风险, stages two real sides

**公式**：强势方 + 强动作 + 弱势方 / 对手 + 反应

**Core logic**: stage two real, named parties in visible confrontation — conflict between real sides is more magnetic than either side's own announcement, because the reader clicks to see who wins.

**Slots**
- 强势方 + 强动作 = a real actor taking a real, checkable aggressive or decisive action (打压、封锁、抢单、施压) — the verb must be strong and specific, not vague friction.
- 弱势方 / 对手 + 反应 = the other real, named side's actual reported response (不惯着、正面刚、绝地反击) — never invented drama.

**范例**
- 《外卖差评被商家阴阳怪气怼破防》（14 字，两方都是泛称，只借它示范强动作 + 对方反应；有名有姓的两方更强）

**AI 废稿**：《两家公司出现分歧，行业关注》（13 字）— 两家公司未点名、分歧无具体动作、行业关注是空洞收尾，三个槽位全落空。

**De-AI tactic**: AI softens conflict into 分歧 / 存在差异 / 引发关注. A person names both sides and the actual verb (打、封、抢、怼) — a named fight is what people click for, not an abstract disagreement.

**Redline**: both the action and the reaction must be real and reported — never invent a company's motive, words, or a confrontation that didn't happen. Taking a side is fine; fabricating the other side's reaction is not.

---

## 11. 问题悬念型 — works everywhere, turns a phenomenon into a question the reader already has

**公式**：现象 / 行为 + 反问 / 设问

**Core logic**: name something real, then hand the reader the exact question they were already half-asking themselves — the question format puts the reader inside their own uncertainty instead of telling them the answer.

**Slots**
- 现象 / 行为 = a real, specific thing the reader has seen or done, anchored in the brief — never a hypothetical.
- 反问 / 设问 = the one question that phenomenon actually raises, phrased so the reader wants the body's answer — not a rhetorical question whose answer is already obvious from the wording.

**范例**
- 《为什么技术越进步，我们加班反而越多了？》（19 字）
- 《游戏都免费了，它到底在赚谁的钱？》（16 字）

**AI 废稿**：《这个问题究竟值不值得我们深思呢》（15 字）— no phenomenon named, no real question, just a reflex closer that could follow any topic.

**De-AI tactic**: AI's default question is empty (这值得我们思考吗？ — thinking about what?). A real question names the exact phenomenon first, so the question mark lands on something specific the reader already has an opinion forming about.

**Redline**: the body must directly answer the question raised, not just discuss the topic around it. A question the piece never resolves is a broken promise, same as any other formula's gap.

---

## 12. 机制揭示型 — for 科普、专业、行业研究, promises the why beneath the what

**公式**：表象现象 + 真正驱动力 / 底层逻辑

**Core logic**: promise to explain the structural reason behind a phenomenon the reader has already noticed — not just that something happened, but why it had to happen this way, which is a stronger, rarer promise than a simple fact reveal.

**Slots**
- 表象现象 = the visible fact or number the reader has already seen (涨价、爆红、亏损、涨粉) — must be real and specific.
- 真正驱动力 / 底层逻辑 = the underlying mechanism the body actually explains — a business logic, an incentive structure, a hidden cost — never a restatement of the phenomenon in fancier words.

**范例**
- 《3.3亿人围观豆包收费，但都气错了地方》（19 字）
- 《为什么所有娱乐平台都免费？因为你就是商品》（20 字）

**AI 废稿**：《一文读懂平台免费背后的商业逻辑》（15 字）— 一文读懂 is a reflex opener with no phenomenon named, exactly the word de-ai-writing.md's Rule 2 flags.

**De-AI tactic**: never open with 一文读懂 / 深度解析. Name the specific visible fact first (免费了、亏损却涨价、突然爆红), then promise the mechanism — the reader gets curious about a real thing, not a generic essay topic.

**Redline**: the body must actually supply a mechanism — an incentive, a cost structure, a real explanation. Restating the phenomenon is not a mechanism. If the body can't explain why, use 反常识颠覆型 instead, which only needs to name the reversal, not prove the mechanism.

## 13. 实体锚定型 — a recognized name is the fastest way into search

**公式**
- `知名实体 + 做了什么 + 为什么重要`
- `实体 A + 对上 + 实体 B`

**Core logic**: a reader-recognized name lowers the cost of understanding to zero before they've read a word — the entity itself is the hook, the action is the payload.

**Slots**
- 知名实体 = a brand, person, or product the reader already recognizes without explanation.
- 做了什么 = a specific, checkable action — never a vague 有动作.
- 为什么重要 = the line that turns "he did X" into "this matters to you" — the slot that separates a headline from a title the reader has a reason to open.

**范例**
- 《微信这波更新，让我连“装死”的资格都没了》（20 字，为什么重要 written as a personal cost）
- 《腾讯：AI管饱，大伙可劲造😂》（14 字，实体 + 动作 + 一句口语收尾）

**Redline**: the entity must be central to the body, not name-dropped for reach.

---

## 14. 强时效型 — turn "happening now" into the reason to click

**公式**：`刚刚 / 今天凌晨 + 实体 + 关键动作`

**前提**：仅当简报里的时效等级 = 即时热点才能用；旧闻、预告、未证实的消息不能装成「刚刚」。

**Core logic**: "this is happening right now" is itself a reason to stop scrolling — timeliness substitutes for depth, but only while the news is actually fresh.

**Slots**
- 刚刚 / 今天凌晨 = the timestamp word, used only when it is literally true.
- 实体 = the real actor the news is about.
- 关键动作 = the one specific thing that just happened — thin on its own, which is why 强时效 titles stay short and spend any spare characters on one closer rather than on adjectives.

**范例**
- 《刚刚！荣耀官宣首次实现全品牌互联》（16 字，timestamp + entity + action）
- 《刚刚，苹果官宣秋季发布会时间》（14 字，the bare version — nothing after the action）

**Redline**: old news, previews, and unconfirmed reports may never be dressed as 刚刚 or 官宣. Only 时效等级 = 即时热点 unlocks this.

---

## 15. 亲测态度转变型 — real experience buys credibility

**公式**：`体验 / 实测 X 后，我改变了看法`

**前提**：必须有真实的体验或试用过程 — 没有实际用过，就不能写「实测」「用了一个月」。

**Core logic**: a documented reversal of the writer's own opinion buys trust no third-person claim can — the reader believes it precisely because it costs the writer something to admit.

**Slots**
- 体验 / 实测 X = the real product or method actually tried, named specifically.
- 我改变了看法 = the concrete before/after of the opinion, stated as a specific admission (我承认…), not a vague 有了新认识.

**范例**
- 《买空净被笑冤大头？算完这笔账我沉默了…》（19 字，被嘲笑的旧判断 + 算账之后的转变）
- 《工作这么多年，我终于学会不讨好面试官了》（19 字，转变来自亲历，不是一件产品的试用）

**Redline**: no 实测 or 用了一个月 without the actual trial behind it.

## 16. 用户原话型 — lift the complaint verbatim, keep the quotes

**公式**：`「一句会脱口而出的抱怨原话」`

**前提**：引用的话必须是真实说过的，或正文里确实出现过 — 不能替读者编一句抱怨。

**Core logic**: the exact words a reader has thought but never said out loud produce instant recognition — the title doesn't describe the frustration, it quotes it verbatim.

**Slots**
- 抱怨原话 = a real line pulled straight from the body or a real complaint, kept in quotation marks, never smoothed into proper written Chinese.
- 收尾（when under budget）= a short close that turns the quote into a promise — 我真的会谢 — needed because a real quote alone often lands under 13 characters.
- 情绪惊叹变体 = `救命 / 天呐 / 谁懂啊 / 真的会谢 + [对象] + [强反应]` — write the reader's first reaction to the content directly into the title. Alone it is empty: pure emotion words carry no search value, so always pair one with a concrete object or result (stack with 搜索词卡位型, #20).

**范例**
- 《谁懂啊！神奇省钱平替网购小技巧！》（16 字，quote-style opener + the thing solved）
- 《救命！这蓬松锁骨发真的会谢！》（14 字）
- 《救命！又遇到骗子了…我真的会谢》（15 字，the close lifts the exclamation past the 13 floor）

**Redline**: the quote must have been actually said or appear in the body. Never invent testimony.

## 17. 场景画面型 — visualized action instead of description

**公式**：`从[具体痛苦动作] + 到[新动作]`

**场景具体化变体**：`[具体时间 / 地点 / 困境] + [依然做到了什么]` — write one life picture into the title and the reader steps into it (`加班到10点还能吃口热的🍜`, `出租屋厨房，也能做出这一桌`).

**Core logic**: a visualized before/after action lets the reader see themselves in the scene instead of being told a benefit exists — a picture where a claim would otherwise stand alone.

**Slots**
- [具体痛苦动作] = a real, physical, relatable action the old way required — 疯狂翻表、手动查资料.
- [新动作] = the equally concrete new action that replaces it — 群里直接 @AI, never an abstract 更高效.

**范例**
- 《下班前5分钟，AI把乱文件夹理干净》（17 字）
- 《谁懂😭上千封邮件再也不用手动整理啦！》（18 字）
- 《上班很累🙈试试在工位养自己》（13 字）
- 《加班到九点回家，减脂晚餐这么吃真不暴食！》（20 字，场景具体化变体：时间 + 困境 + 依然做到）
- 《在宁波加班到深夜，全靠这口烟火气续命🔥》（19 字，场景具体化变体：地点 + 时间 + 一口具体的吃食）

**Redline**: the scene is real; do not invent a persona's experience.

## 18. 对话剧本型 — a reversal staged as dialogue, colons splitting the roles

**公式**：`[权威角色]：「[刁难]」，我：「[回应]」`

**前提**：必须有真实的交锋经历，或明确标注是戏剧化改编 — 不能凭空编一段对话。

**Core logic**: staging the exchange as dialogue puts the reader inside the confrontation in real time, instead of summarizing that a confrontation happened.

**Slots**
- [权威角色] + [刁难] = a real person in a position of judgment, and the actual challenge they posed.
- 我 + [回应] = the writer's real comeback — the reversal lives in this half, so it has to land, not just close the exchange politely.

**范例**
- None yet: the only near miss, 《面试官问：你的项目为什么非要用Agent？》, stages the interviewer's question but not the writer's reply.

**This structure is the greediest one here.** Quotation marks, role colons, and two speakers cost 6–8 characters before any content. Fitting inside 20 usually means giving up the third beat (the other side's reaction), which is where the reversal lives — so use it only when the exchange itself is strong enough to work in two beats.

**Redline**: real experience, or labeled clearly as dramatization.

## 19. 社会证明型 — an early-adopter group's checkable result, not manufactured excitement

**公式**：`第一批 / 最先 + 谁 + 已经 + 结果`

**Core logic**: a real early-adopter group with a checkable outcome makes the reader fear being left behind — without a single exclamation point doing the manufacturing.

**Slots**
- 第一批 / 最先 + 谁 = a real, specific group who acted early — 第一批用上XX的人、最先转型的团队 — never a vague 很多人.
- 已经 + 结果 = a checkable outcome that group already has, stated plainly, so the reader can compare their own position to it.

**范例**
- 《第一批用上AI的洋牛马，已经踏入巅峰》（18 字）
- 《第一批用对精华的油敏皮已经上岸了》（16 字）
- 《第一批用AI的人，开始后悔了》（14 字，the outcome runs negative — the same slot works for a warning）

**AI 废稿**：《很多姐妹已经开始用这个好物了》（14 字）— 很多人是泛群体，方法未点名，没有可验证的结果，槽位全空。

**Redline**: 第一批 and the outcome must be literally true — the same discipline as the 从众 trigger's own Redline in `references/psych-triggers.md`. Never invent an early-adopter group or a result the body doesn't show.

## 20. 搜索词卡位型 — the words a user types are the trunk of the title

**公式**：`[核心搜索词] + [限定条件] + [差异化后缀]`

**Core logic**: 小红书 keeps sending search traffic to a note long after the feed has moved on, so the trunk of the title should be what a reader types into the search box — usually 品类 + 场景 + 修饰 — not a literary phrase.

**Slots**
- 核心搜索词 = one term a reader would actually type, written in full (宿舍桌面改造, 油皮粉底液). One well-aimed term beats three stacked.
- 限定条件 = the scene, person, or budget that narrows it (夏天不脱妆、百元、周末).
- 差异化后缀 = the one thing that makes this note not a copy of the ten above it (不累版、抄作业).

**范例**
- 《大学宿舍床上布置‖打造自己的温馨小屋》（18 字，品类 + 场景 as the trunk, the 后缀 carries the pitch）
- 《准大一抄作业✍️宿舍桌面改造💡百元搞定💯》（19 字，搜索词 + 预算 + 抄作业）
- 《我亲测走过的8条上海citywalk路线》（20 字，地点 + 搜索词 + 数字，「我亲测走过」 is the differentiating suffix）
- 《上海一日游攻略✔️经典citywalk路线》（20 字，两个可搜索的词并排，经典 限定）

**AI 废稿**：《这个夏天的美好小物分享给大家》（14 字）— no category, no scene, nothing a person would type. A mood is not a search term.

**De-AI tactic**: write the phrase a stranger would type when they have exactly this problem, then add the one qualifier only this note has. A pretty phrase that nobody searches is invisible.

**Redline**: no hot word unrelated to the body. Piling irrelevant keywords onto a title is judged as marketing behavior and throttled.

## 21. 避雷劝退型 — say what not to do

**公式**：`[品类 / 行为] + 别买 / 别踩 / 千万别 + [代价]` 或 `我踩过的[N]个坑`

**前提**：a named negative verdict must come from your own experience. If nobody actually used the product, write a generic pitfall of the category and name no one.

**Core logic**: 「别做什么」 out-clicks 「做什么」 — **【经验】** the most reliable family on 小红书 — because it prices the mistake before the reader makes it. It is the sentence shape for the 避坑 trigger in `references/psych-triggers.md`.

**Slots**
- 品类 / 行为 = the concrete thing, never 有些东西.
- 别买 / 别踩 / 千万别 = the verdict, in the reader's own register.
- 代价 = what ignoring it costs (买完后悔四年) — the slot that separates a warning from a slogan.

**范例**
- 《大一入住｜宿舍坏物避雷❌快看看你的购物车》（20 字）
- 《这4类网红文具❗真心不建议小学生购买》（18 字）
- 《宿舍8大智商税‼️买完后悔四年》（14 字，数字聚焦 stacked）

**AI 废稿**：《这些产品不推荐购买，大家一定要注意》（17 字）— no category, no cost, and 大家一定要注意 warns of nothing.

**De-AI tactic**: name the category and the price of buying it; state the verdict as your own experience (我踩过) rather than a rule handed down.

**Redline**: a negative claim about a named brand must be personal experience, framed as subjective. Never fabricate a defect and never imply a brand fakes anything (legal risk).

## 22. 后悔体型 — 相见恨晚 signals scarce information

**公式**：`后悔 / 居然 / 竟然 + 没早知道 + [具体的事]`

**前提**：the body must add real information. Common knowledge under 「后悔没早知道」 is clickbait.

**Core logic**: the regret of 「早知道就好了」 tells the reader this is information they are still missing.

**Slots**
- 后悔 / 居然 / 竟然 = the regret or surprise word.
- 没早知道 = the time gap that makes the information feel scarce.
- 具体的事 = the named thing. Without it the title is a placeholder.

**范例**
- 《后悔没早点知道的新媒体运营流程》（15 字，具体的事 named in full）
- 《后悔没早点知道！GTM全流程拆解》（16 字）
- 《避雷！考研期间最后悔买的东西...》（17 字，the regret is over a purchase rather than a missing fact — 避雷劝退型 stacked）

The bare wording (`后悔没早点知道这个方法😭`) runs only 12 characters, so it needs 具体的事 spelled out to clear the 13 floor — which is what the real titles above do.

**AI 废稿**：《后悔没早点知道这个方法，真的太重要了》（18 字）— 这个方法 is never named, and 真的太重要了 fills the space where the information should be.

**De-AI tactic**: replace 这个方法 with the method. If it cannot be named in a phrase, the piece has no 信息增量 to be regretful about.

**Redline**: the body has to deliver information the reader likely did not have.

## 23. 亲测对比型 — visible change is the hardest hook in 种草

**公式**：`[时长 / 动作] 后，[可观测的变化]` 或 `[变量A] vs [变量B]`

**前提**：no 亲测, 坚持了 X 个月, or 实测 without a real trial behind it — the same hard filter as 亲测态度转变型.

**Core logic**: 亲测态度转变型 (#15) sells a changed opinion; this one sells a changed *observable* — a before/after or an A vs B the reader can see for themselves.

**Slots**
- 时长 / 动作 = how long, or what was done, in numbers.
- 可观测的变化 = what a camera could show. Describe the effect as your own feeling, not a therapeutic result.
- 变量 A vs B = when there is no time axis, two things compared on one dimension.

**范例**
- 《两件urban同款卫衣实测对比》（15 字，A vs B）
- 《停了兴趣班一年后的三个变化（无滤镜）》（18 字，时长 + 变化）

**AI 废稿**：《用了一段时间之后，效果真的很明显》（16 字）— no duration, nothing observable, and 很明显 asserts what the title should show.

**De-AI tactic**: put the duration and the changed thing in the title as nouns and numbers; leave the adjective 明显 for the reader to conclude.

**Redline**: write effects as your own experience and never as a 疗效 (see the 医疗与功效断言 redline in `references/scoring-and-redlines.md`).

## 24. 保姆级教程型 — a zero-barrier promise aimed at 「我也想会」

**公式**：`零基础 / 新手 / 小白 + [目标] + 保姆级 / N 步搞定`

**前提**：the body must be real step-by-step, and its step count must match the title. If no actionable steps can be given, do not use 保姆级.

**Core logic**: it promises a path the reader can copy, aimed straight at the people who want to learn it.

**Slots**
- 零基础 / 新手 / 小白 = the entry level, which doubles as the 人群 tag.
- 目标 = the concrete skill or output.
- 保姆级 / N 步搞定 = the promise. Prefer a number over the adjective: 3步 is checkable, 保姆级 is a claim.

**范例**
- 《保姆级教学😭0基础3步搞定阅读写作！》（18 字）
- 《新手vlog剪辑｜一招搞定丝滑无缝转场》（19 字，新手 + 目标 + 搞定，步数落在「一招」上）
- 《剪映全套保姆教程｜新手直接抄作业》（16 字，保姆级 with a copy-it cue）

**AI 废稿**：《新手入门必备教程，轻松学会所有技能》（17 字）— 所有技能 names no goal, and 必备 and 轻松 are the stock words the de-AI gate flags.

**De-AI tactic**: 搞定 is on Rule 2's caution list, so let it point at a number (3步搞定) and a concrete target, never at 一切.

**Redline**: the step count in the title must equal the steps in the body.

## 25. 平替省钱型 — a money benefit, stated in numbers

**公式**：`[预算数字] + 搞定 / 买齐 + [目标]` 或 `[贵价对象] 的平替`

**前提**：every price must be real and carry its date or channel basis. 平替 is a subjective judgment of similarity — never claim 效果相同 or 成分一致 to a bigger brand, which invites a false-advertising finding.

**Core logic**: **【经验】** a clear money benefit is one of the most frequent consumption motives on 小红书. A budget number turns it from a mood into a decision.

**Slots**
- 预算数字 = the amount the reader can compare to their own.
- 搞定 / 买齐 = the verb that says the whole job fits inside that amount.
- 目标 or 贵价对象 = what was bought, or what it stands in for.

**范例**
- 《买嵌入式直饮机超预算？我直接冲平替》（17 字）
- 《低至4r近期很满意的平价实用物合集》（17 字）
- 《百来块出头用出了贵妇级的体验》（14 字，预算数字 + 体验，写的是自己的感受）
- 《家里这10样东西，平替就够了》（14 字，贵价对象的平替）

**AI 废稿**：《省钱好物推荐，性价比超高值得入手》（16 字）— no amount, no target, and 性价比超高 is the claim the number should prove.

**De-AI tactic**: state the amount and the thing it bought. Let the reader do the 性价比 arithmetic.

**Redline**: no absolute price promise without its date or channel; no 效果相同 or 成分一致.

## 26. 求助提问型 — a sincere question traded for comments

**公式**：`[具体选择困境] + 在线等 / 求推荐 / 怎么选`

**前提**：the body must be able to carry the discussion. Do not ask and then answer it yourself.

**Core logic**: a real, specific question invites replies directly. It differs from 问题悬念型 (#11), which withholds an answer the body then gives; here the reader is the one who answers.

**Slots**
- 具体选择困境 = the actual either/or or the situation, specific enough to answer in one line.
- 在线等 / 求推荐 / 怎么选 = the call to reply.

**范例**
- 《大家last day要请同事喝奶茶吗》（18 字）
- 《你们会主动找老公要零花钱吗？》（14 字）
- 《一人说一个大学不建议买的东西！！》（16 字，an open call rather than a single question）

**AI 废稿**：《大家怎么看这个问题呢？欢迎留言讨论》（17 字）— no problem named, and 欢迎留言讨论 asks for nothing anyone can answer.

**De-AI tactic**: name the exact dilemma, small enough that a stranger can answer in one line.

**Redline**: **【经验】** these titles get high interaction and low search value, so they should not be an account's main line.

---

### Use-with-care set
High tension, fastest to burn trust. Only when the body holds them up: **极值** (`全球首个` — banned without a source), **时代拔高** (`一个时代结束了` — not for every minor update), **恐惧威胁** (`最可怕的 AI 已经来了`, `不看这条你会后悔一辈子` — over-intimidation is judged as clickbait), **告别情感** (`永别了，豆包` — ironic), **拟人自述** (`一个大模型的自述`), **绝对化拔高** (`最好用的XX` — 小红书 reviews absolutes more strictly than 公众号 did, and 最 / 第一 / 顶级 / 销量冠军 readily trigger throttling, so avoid them unless necessary), **数据炫耀** (`月入5万的副业` — income promises are a priority audit target, so phrase them as personal experience).

## 加辣 enhancers

Apply candidate by candidate; they stack. **Seasoning never adds a fact** — it changes the expression, never the content: nothing goes into the title that the body does not contain. Re-measure after seasoning, since every one of these costs characters and the ceiling is 20. Step 8.2 in `SKILL.md` sizes each change against the room the draft leaves, using the costs below; an enhancer with no cost noted swaps words and adds 0 to 2 characters.

1. **时间前置** — 刚刚 / 今天凌晨 / 别睡了 at the head (+2 to +4 chars). Only with 时效等级 = 即时热点.
2. **热词前置** — put the entity people are currently searching at the head of the sentence.
3. **具体化换抽象** — 效率提升 → 干完一个小团队的活; 便宜 → 30 块; 很多 → 7 个. Usually **adds** characters, and is almost always worth them. The number must already be in the brief.
4. **极端化限定词** — 最容易踩的坑 / 彻底告别. **Check the compliance gate first**: 最好 / 史上最 / 国家级 are eliminated outright, and 第一 / 唯一 need a human ruling — so keep the intensity in the verb (彻底告别) rather than in a superlative.
5. **身份反差** — let the person who should not be saying this say it: 奶奶、医生、面试官、CEO — 我妈看完直接下单了, 理发师朋友劝我别买. The contrasting identity must really exist in the body; never invent a persona (+2 to +4 chars).
6. **口语加语气** — 啊 / 吗 / 了 and colloquial word swaps, to break 书面腔: 提升效率 → 不用加班了; 具有性价比 → 便宜到离谱 (+1 to +3 chars).
7. **省略号留白** — a trailing … for something left unsaid (+1 char; counts as a decorative mark in the de-AI gate, so do not combine it with a colon and an exclamation mark).
8. **符号分区** — **【经验】** on 小红书 the most common divider is ｜ (`学生党｜宿舍好物`), then 感叹号, 问号, and a bracketed complement; the colon is used less often. Use ｜ as 场景｜结果: two segments of information at a glance, the most typical 小红书 layout. Each symbol costs 1 character.
9. **人群词前置** — open with the group's own tag: 学生党、i人、小个子、新手妈妈. The tag must name the audience the body really serves (see 痛点直击型's 标签前置变体) (+2 to +4 chars).
10. **emoji 定调** — 1–2 emoji at the end of the title set the mood: 😭 崩溃 / ✨ 好物 / 📌 收藏 / 🔥 热度 / 👀 好奇. Never more than 3. Each one spends a character of the 20.
11. **搜索词补位** — check the title for a noun a user would really search; if there is none, swap one adjective out and put that noun in.
12. **收藏钩子加尾** — end with 建议收藏 / 码住 / 照抄就行 (+3 to +4 chars), only when the content is genuinely reusable.
13. **第一人称化** — turn a statement into your own experience: 「这样做更好」 → 「我这样做之后…」. The experience must be real (+1 to +2 chars).
14. **原话加引号** — move the line a reader would blurt out — a complaint or an exclamation — into the title verbatim, in quotation marks (`「这也太难删了吧！」`). Quotes carry the sense of someone speaking that a paraphrase loses. The line must have been really heard or appear in the body; never invent a user's testimony (see 用户原话型). The quotation marks cost 2 characters; the line itself usually replaces words already in the title.

## Formula stacking

The formulas are not mutually exclusive, and 爆款 titles are regularly two of them layered:

- 《月薪5k到5万，我只做对了这3件事》= 数字聚焦 + 悬念钩子
- 《3.3亿人围观豆包收费，但都气错了地方》= 实体锚定 + 反常识颠覆
- 《每天10分钟，四个动作无痛直腿养成计划》= 利益前置 + 数字聚焦

**Two at most.** Three formulas inside 20 characters is the 模板拼凑 failure — every part present, nothing landing.
