# Writing titles that don't read as AI-generated

Read this **before** drafting a single candidate at Step 7, not after. These are not a checklist to run against a finished title — no script checks any of them, and none of them can be. They are how a person who writes 小红书 titles for a living actually thinks while writing one. Internalize the instinct now, so the first draft of every candidate already sounds like it, instead of writing the AI-default version and patching it afterward.

If you do catch a candidate lapsing into one of these patterns — during generation, or while rewriting a user's existing title at Step 3 — fix it and say **specifically what changed** (原文 → 改后), e.g. 「把『新手妈妈的情绪压力』换成一句能被截图的话」. Never write 「去掉了 AI 味」. If a title was already natural, write 「无，原始生成已自然」.

**About the ❌/✅ pairs below**: every ✅ is a real 小红书 title from `references/title-formulas.md`; every ❌ is a synthetic failure written for this page, at a length that passes the gate — so the gap between the two halves is never a length gap. Read that gap; it is the entire lesson.

### Rule 1 — spoken over written
AI **names** a feeling or a state — 压力、责任、重要性 — instead of showing it. A person writes the scene or the verb that the feeling actually lives in, not the abstract noun for it.
- ❌ 《新手妈妈的情绪压力与调适方法》（14 字）— vague label, abstract emotion, no line anyone would screenshot. 压力 and 调适 are an emotion and a fix **named** rather than felt.
- ✅ 《当妈哪有不疯的？又哪有不会变好的？》（17 字）

**【经验】On 小红书 this goes further: strong first person, strong colloquial.** 我、姐妹们、谁懂啊、救命、真的会谢 are native there; a written-language tone reads as a signal to leave.

### Rule 2 — use words commonly used in AI with caution
These words are not banned. AI just reaches for them by default, out of habit, without asking whether this exact sentence needs them. A person can use any of them — the difference is a person only reaches for one when it is actually the right word for this one line, not as a reflex. Before using one, ask: **would a person pick this word here?**

```
深入、关键、核心、必备、必看、搞定、轻松、快速、高效、提升、打造、解锁、分享、推荐、建议、究竟、到底、背后、干货、赋能、助力、一文读懂、全面解析、值得拥有、的重要性
```

- ❌ 《深度解析护肤背后的底层逻辑，建议收藏》（18 字）— 深度解析 and 建议收藏 here are reflex, not choice: nothing in this specific sentence needed them.
- ✅ 《网购换这些关键词，直接省一半！》（15 字）

揭秘 is not off-limits — it is legitimate whenever the piece really does open a mechanism. The test is the same one above: does the word point at something specific, or is it just decoration? Apply that question to every word on the list, case by case, not as a blanket avoidance rule.

### Rule 3 — lock a concrete audience, not a demographic label
AI reaches for the group name — 职场人、宝妈、年轻人 — and stops there, as if naming the demographic were the same as speaking to it. A person names the behavior that group is actually doing right now: not 打工人 but 天天加班到 10 点还被骂效率低的打工人. The reader's first reaction has to be 「这说的不就是我吗」, not 「这好像跟我有点关系」.
- ❌ 《应届生必看的求职心态调整指南》（14 字）— 泛人群、无痛点、无预期，三个槽位全空；必看 和 指南 只是把字数垫进区间。字数进了区间从来不说明槽位满了。
- ✅ 《24毕业gap两年找不到工作每天都想哭》（19 字）

**【经验】小红书 identity tags are the exception, and they go up front:** 学生党、i人、小个子、新手妈妈、油皮、通勤党、预算党. These are labels readers already call themselves, so within half a second they confirm 「说的是我」. Lead with the tag and let the scene or payoff follow it; do not stack several tags, and do not invent a tag the piece just coined.

### Rule 4 — hold a position, don't report
AI writes reversals that are fake — 「你以为 A，其实是 B」 where B is just another piece of correct-sounding filler, safe enough that no one is offended by it. A real position costs the reader something to accept: it has to contradict what they did yesterday, what they just paid for, what they already believe.
- ❌ 《理财的重要性：如何提升你的收入》（15 字）— runs with the consensus, no conflict, no reason to click. 的重要性 and 如何 are the two most reliable markers of a title with no position in it.
- ✅ 《不敢赚钱，因为你一直在逃避赚钱》（15 字）

### Rule 5 — a number needs a partner, not a size
AI reaches for one big, round number and calls it a hook — 10 个技巧、15 个方法 — and the bigger it is, the more it reads as padding. A real number technique is a **gap between two numbers**: one for temptation, one for feasibility. The distance between them is the reason to click, not either number alone.
- ❌ 《提升自我的10个实用方法，建议收藏》（17 字）— the number hangs on an empty concept, no value, no suspense, and 10 个 is the exact size AI defaults to.
- ✅ 《月薪5k到5万，我只做对了这3件事》（17 字）— the big number is the temptation, the small one is the feasibility, and the distance between them is the reason to click.

### Rule 6 — the gap has to be over something the reader wants
AI writes flat narration and hides a generic takeaway behind it — 「才明白这个道理」 — as if withholding a moral were the same thing as suspense. A real gap withholds a specific, wanted answer: an action, a number, a result, not a lesson.
- ❌ 《面试失败3次后，我才明白这个道理》（16 字）— in budget and still dead: no emotion, no conflict, and 这个道理 withholds nothing anybody wanted. Hitting the character budget is a gate, not an achievement.
- ✅ 《当着办公室所有人的面骂了业务面试官》（17 字）

### The standard above all of them
**Read it out loud. Does it sound like a line a friend sent you?** If not, rewrite it.
