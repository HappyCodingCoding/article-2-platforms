# Trigger layer — the 10 psychological mechanisms on 公众号

The formula decides the sentence shape. The trigger decides **which nerve it touches and what the reader does next**. Human psychology does not vary by platform; what varies is how a mechanism lands. 公众号 gives you 20–26 characters and a subscription list — enough to finish a causal sentence, and no search long tail to protect. So on 公众号 a mechanism can be argued; on 小红书 the same mechanism has to be compressed into keywords.

One title carries **one primary trigger, at most one secondary.** A trigger expresses what the content already is. If the piece has no 希望感 in it, do not bolt 希望 onto the title — the reader finds out within one screen and the account pays for it.

## Action → trigger lookup

| Reader action | Priority triggers |
|---|---|
| 点击 | 好奇、捷径、悬念 |
| 转发 | 态度、共愤、幽默、从众 |
| 评论 | 共愤、避坑、态度 |
| 收藏 | 清单、希望、避坑、治愈 |

---

## 1. 好奇

**Basis**: the brain resents an information gap. The gap pulls hardest when it is specific — not "you don't know something" but "you know 80% and are missing the 20% that matters" (Loewenstein's information-gap theory).

**Action**: 点击.

**Content prerequisite**: there is one thing in the body the reader probably does not know and would consider worth knowing. The title implies it exists and withholds the conclusion.

**公众号 landing**: the budget is enough to write 现象 + 为什么 as a full sentence and hang the gap on the back half of the causal chain — 《为什么你的时间管理一直没用？问题出在你没注意的这步》.

**词库**：秘密、内幕、真相、原因、终于知道、原来、居然、才发现、问题出在

**Redline**: the body must start filling the hole in its first paragraph. A gap that turns out to contain common knowledge costs more trust than the click was worth.

## 2. 捷径

**Basis**: the contrast (笨 ↔ 管用, 变态 ↔ 有效) produces "I don't believe it but I want to try", stacked on top of the oldest wish there is — maximum result for minimum effort. 好奇 + 希望 layered.

**Action**: 点击 + 收藏.

**Content prerequisite**: a genuinely non-standard method that works — something figured out, not the textbook answer. **This is a gate, not a coat of paint.** Most content does not qualify.

**公众号 landing**: front half lowers expectations with a contrast label, back half raises them with a result, and the budget leaves room for one qualifying condition — 《一个很笨的方法，我靠它把周报时间砍掉了三分之二》.

**词库**：反差标签（笨、变态、偏方、野路子、懒人、反直觉）；结果（能上岸、能提效、能省下）；捷径（速成、抄作业、一步到位）

**Redline**: match the contrast label to the account's persona — a 知识号 uses 笨 or 反直觉, not 变态.

## 3. 从众

**Basis**: high-arousal emotion travels further than low-arousal emotion (Berger, STEPPS). "Everyone is already on this" activates fear of missing out.

**Action**: 转发 / 转化.

**Content prerequisite**: the thing is genuinely good enough to push hard. Manufactured excitement is transparent.

**公众号 landing**: 公众号 readers tolerate exclamation stacking far less than 小红书 readers do. Use the **社会证明 variant** rather than raw exclamation — 《第一批用上XX的人，已经把这活儿甩掉一周了》.

**词库**：第一批、已经、都在用、人手一个、卖爆了、我宣布

**Redline**: 第一批 has to be literally true. Social proof invented for the title is a fabricated fact.

## 4. 避坑

**Basis**: three effects stacked — 损失厌恶 (losing hurts roughly twice as much as gaining pleases; Kahneman), 后悔规避 (「后悔没早知道」 implies they are losing right now without realizing), and 逆权威信任 (someone telling you *not* to buy reads as one of us, not as an advertiser).

**Action**: 收藏 + 关注.

**Content prerequisite**: real experience of the pit — theirs or one they watched enough others fall into. **劝退 without an alternative is half a piece of content.**

**公众号 landing**: the budget fits 劝退 + 替代 in one line — 《别再买这类课了，同样的钱买这三样，我帮你算过账》. The 逆权威 posture must match the actual weight of the content; an ordinary review does not get 得罪人也要说.

**词库**：别买、别再、真的没必要、说句大实话、后悔没早点、白花了、不如

**Redline**: named negative claims about a specific brand must be personal experience, clearly framed as subjective. Never allege fabrication, toxicity, or harm without proof.

## 5. 共愤

**Basis**: someone wronged does not primarily want a solution, they want company in the anger. Shared anger builds in-group identity — you become their 嘴替 and the comment section becomes a queue of people with the same story.

**Action**: 评论 + 关注.

**Content prerequisite**: a real negative experience. Readers can tell a genuine grievance from a performed one.

**公众号 landing**: name the target concretely (a behavior, a practice, a phenomenon — not 生活好难) and leave an opening for the reader to answer into. End the body with an exit; do not send them away holding the anger.

**词库**：心态崩了、太下头了、无语、气笑了、谁能管管、能不能别、终于有人说了

**Redline**: no 性别对立, no 地域攻击, no group stigma. Anger at a practice, never at a population.

## 6. 治愈

**Basis**: chronic anxiety and isolation create demand for a psychological safe base — being understood and accepted (attachment theory). The exact opposite of 从众: not excitement, but calm. These get saved because of how they make the reader feel, not because they are useful.

**Action**: 收藏 + 关注.

**Content prerequisite**: the content actually is warm. Warmth is a quality, not a word choice.

**公众号 landing**: 公众号 is unusually good for this — evening subscription reading is exactly the context. The title needs **one concrete tender detail**: a scene, a sentence, a moment. 《下班后的那一个小时，是我一天里不用回消息的时候》. Empty 加油 and 你一定可以 irritate rather than soothe.

**词库**：一个人、深夜、下班后、慢下来、不着急、没关系、辛苦了、原来我不是一个人

**Redline**: do not sell comfort the body does not deliver.

## 7. 幽默

**Basis**: humor is benign violation — an expectation broken with no real threat. 社死 and 翻车 stories release the reader's own "I do stupid things too" pressure. Forwarding something funny is social currency.

**Action**: 转发 + 点赞.

**Content prerequisite**: it is actually funny. Forced humor is the most uncomfortable content there is.

**公众号 landing**: set up an expectation and imply the result went badly — 《我发誓我只是想做个饭，最后是消防员帮我关的火》. **Self-deprecation ranks far above mocking anyone else.**

**词库**：社死现场、笑不活了、离了大谱、当我试图、结果、迷惑行为

**Redline**: no 擦边, no personal attacks. The body has to carry the joke — a punchline title over an instruction-manual body is worse than no joke.

## 8. 希望

**Basis**: readers project themselves into a success story and buy the feeling of "I could do that too" (possible selves). Upward comparison gives vicarious satisfaction without having to arrive.

**Action**: 收藏 + 转发.

**Content prerequisite**: a perceivable change (瘦了 15 斤、上岸、把周报时间砍掉三分之二) or an enviable state. **Quantify it** — 瘦了 is nothing next to 瘦了 15 斤.

**公众号 landing**: the budget fits 起点 → 终点 → 关键动作, which is exactly the 数字聚焦 + 悬念 stack — 《从月薪3千到月入5万，我其实只做对了2件事》.

**词库**：上岸了、终于、普通人也能、从 0 到 1、我只做对了、是种什么体验

**Redline**: 月入过万 as a promise of reproducible income is a compliance failure. Personal experience, no guarantee. The body must carry the real method or the real detail.

## 9. 态度

**Basis**: two sub-mechanisms. **温柔反叛** — 「一定要 X 吗，可我 Y」 is gentle resistance to a social script: not angry, just self-accepting. **身份标签** — a reader forwards 「勇敢的人先享受世界」 to declare *I am that kind of person*. The forwarding motive is not "this is useful" but "this represents me", which is the highest form of social currency and the highest forward rate of any trigger here.

**Action**: 转发 + 评论.

**Content prerequisite**: the piece genuinely holds a position, supported by real experience rather than slogan.

**公众号 landing**: this is where 公众号 beats every other platform — the subscription relationship *is* a values relationship. Anchor a specific script being refused (一定要考公吗、一定要合群吗) and declare the choice.

**词库**：一定要 X 吗、可我、那又怎样、我就这样、先享受世界、真的很有魅力

**Redline**: rebellion stays gentle — self-acceptance, not attack on people who chose differently.

## 10. 清单

**Basis**: in an overloaded feed, "I filtered it for you" is itself the value. A list title becomes a **savable asset** — the reader is not saving an article, they are saving an organized set.

**Action**: 收藏 + 点击.

**Content prerequisite**: the content was genuinely filtered. 「我看了 50 个，挑了 5 个」 is worth far more than 「我列了 5 个」.

**公众号 landing**: specific number + specific scene + a curation signal, and the budget still leaves room for a payoff — 《面试前一晚该看的5件事，我踩过的坑都在里面》. Note that 必看 and 干货 are exactly the stock words the de-AI gate flags, so carry the curation signal in the verb instead.

**词库**：精选、私藏、整理、直接抄、照着做、我踩过的坑、我挑剩下的

**Redline**: the count in the title must equal the count in the body. Small and filtered beats large and padded.
