# Writing titles that don't read as AI-generated

Read this **before** drafting a single candidate at Step 6, not after. These are not a checklist to run against a finished title — no script checks any of them, and none of them can be. They are how a person who writes 公众号 titles for a living actually thinks while writing one. Internalize the instinct now, so the first draft of every candidate already sounds like it, instead of writing the AI-default version and patching it afterward.

If you do catch a candidate lapsing into one of these patterns — during generation, or while rewriting a user's existing title at Step 3 — fix it and say **specifically what changed** (原文 → 改后), e.g. 「把『轻松搞定』换成『不用愁』」. Never write 「去掉了 AI 味」. If a title was already natural, write 「无，原始生成已自然」.

**About the ❌/✅ pairs below**: each one isolates a single variable, so both halves are stripped of everything else — most of them are 11–18 characters and would not survive the length gate as they stand. They are demonstrations of one move, not shippable titles. Copy the move, not the length; the 20–26 gate is applied separately at Step 8 of the workflow.

### Rule 1 — kill the assembled-template feel
AI jams several template parts into one line and it reads like a checklist. A human title is **one thought coming out**, not a form being filled.
- ❌ 《3 个方法轻松搞定 XX，新手必看！别再踩坑了！》— number template + 轻松搞定 + audience tag + pit warning, four parts bolted together
- ✅ 《我踩了 3 次坑才学会的 XX 方法》

### Rule 2 — break the perfect sentence
AI writes complete, tidy, grammatically flawless sentences. Real titles are fragments, exclamations, half-thoughts.
- ❌ 《这是我经过多次尝试后发现的最高效的学习方法》
- ✅ 《试了无数种，就这个方法管用》

### Rule 3 — compress AI's stock vocabulary
These words are not wrong; AI just reaches for them mechanically. When one appears, ask: **would a person pick this word here?**

深入、关键、核心、必备、必看、搞定、轻松、快速、高效、提升、打造、解锁、分享、推荐、建议、究竟、到底、背后、干货、赋能、助力、一文读懂、全面解析、值得拥有、的重要性

- ❌ 《揭秘 XX 背后的底层逻辑，建议收藏》
- ✅ 《XX 的真相：没那么复杂》

（揭秘 is legitimate when the piece really does open a mechanism — the test is whether it points at something specific or is just decoration.）

### Rule 4 — spoken over written
- ❌ 《有效提升工作效率的 5 个关键策略》
- ✅ 《不想加班？练这 5 个习惯》

### Rule 5 — no number added just to have a number
List numbers need real content behind them; a number is not an ornament. And the bigger the number, the more it reads as padding — AI reaches for 10 个技巧 and 15 个方法 by default.
- ❌ 《一定要知道的 5 件事》 over a body that never lists five
- ✅ 《改掉这一个习惯，我的周报时间少了一半》

### Rule 6 — go easy on symbols
AI likes colons to segment, dashes to pause, exclamation marks to insist. People use them sparingly and casually. 公众号 corpus: about 28% use a colon, about 20% an exclamation mark — stacking them reads cheap.
- ❌ 《效率提升：3 个你必须知道的方法！建议收藏！》
- ✅ 《效率翻倍的 3 个方法，很实用》

### Rule 7 — allow imperfection
Real titles invert word order, drop words, and sometimes read slightly rough. That roughness is the human signal.
- ❌ 《自律的人都会做的 5 件事》
- ✅ 《自律的人，都有这 5 个习惯》

### Rule 8 — hold a position, don't report
The title should carry a judgment, not neutrally announce that a method exists.
- ❌ 《适合新手的写作方法分享》
- ✅ 《我试过最笨但最有用的写作方法》

### The standard above all of them
**Read it out loud. Does it sound like a line a friend sent you?** If not, rewrite it.
